What is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai listens to your meetings and writes them down as they happen. Its homepage describes live transcription in multiple languages with speaker recognition, so you can see who said what. An AI Meeting Agent can join your Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet calls automatically from your calendar, so you don't have to remember to press record. If you don't want a bot in the call, the Mac and Windows desktop apps can record straight from your computer.

After the call, Otter writes a summary with decisions, key points and action items. Otter AI Chat then lets you ask questions across your meetings and connected apps. For example, you could ask what the team agreed about a deadline, and then turn the answer into a follow-up email or report.

Key features of Otter.ai

Live transcription with speaker labels

Text appears while people talk and is split by speaker. Otter's pricing page lists AI transcription in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Chinese, plus custom vocabulary for names and terms (5 terms on the free plan).

Automatic summaries and action items

Otter's pricing table lists automated summaries with action items and outlines. This is the main time-saver: you read a short summary instead of a full transcript.

Otter AI Chat

AI Chat works within a single meeting and across all of them. It has monthly limits: 20 questions per user on Basic (3 per conversation), 50 on Pro and 200 on Business and Enterprise.

Integrations

The homepage names Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Notion, Asana, Google Docs and Dropbox among its connected apps, and the Pro plan lists Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier. Action items often end up on a task board. If that's how your team works, our Trello review explains how a simple board tool can take over from there.

Apps for every device

Otter offers desktop apps for Mac and Windows, iOS and Android apps, a Chrome extension and recording on the web.

Otter.ai free plan: what you actually get

The Basic plan is free forever and includes 300 transcription minutes a month, 20 AI Chat questions and the Zoom, Teams and Meet integrations. Its limits are tight:

  • Each conversation stops at 30 minutes.
  • You can import only 3 audio or video files in total, ever.
  • Only your 25 most recent conversations stay in your history.
  • You can join only one meeting at a time.

That works for short check-ins and for testing Otter on a real call. Anyone with hour-long meetings will need Pro (90 minutes per conversation) or Business (4 hours).

Otter.ai pros and cons

Pros

  • A free plan you can keep using, not just a trial
  • Works with Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet
  • Desktop apps record without a bot joining the call
  • Summaries, action items and AI Chat are built in, not add-ons

Cons

  • The 30-minute cap per conversation on the free plan is short
  • Only six transcription languages are listed
  • Regional and time-limited offers make the real price harder to read
  • The import limit for Business is stated in two different ways

Who should use Otter.ai?

A good fit if you:

  • Spend most of your week in video meetings and lose time writing notes
  • Need searchable transcripts and a clear list of next steps after each call
  • Work mainly in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese or Chinese

Look elsewhere if you:

  • Hold long meetings and don't want to pay: the free plan stops at 30 minutes
  • Need transcription in a language Otter doesn't list
  • Need a public price for a large rollout: Enterprise is quote-only

Tips before you sign up

  1. Test on Basic first. Record one real meeting under 30 minutes and check the quality of the transcript and summary.
  2. Check your language. Make sure your team's language is on the list of six.
  3. Compare billing periods. Pro costs about half as much per month with annual billing.
  4. Count your imports. If you transcribe recorded files often, Basic's 3 lifetime imports will run out fast.
  5. Ask about compliance. HIPAA (as an add-on), SSO and SCIM are listed on Enterprise only.

Our verdict

Otter.ai covers the basics of AI meeting notes well on paper. You get live transcripts, automatic summaries, action items and AI Chat, on the three biggest meeting platforms. The free plan is fine for short calls, Pro suits most individuals, and Business is aimed at teams that hold long meetings.
